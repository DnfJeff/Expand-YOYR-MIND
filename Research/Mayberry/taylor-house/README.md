# Taylor House

Home base. The center of everything.

The Taylor house is where Andy, Opie, and Aunt Bee live. It's a modest two-story home on a tree-lined residential street. But the house is really three spaces that serve completely different narrative functions:

| Space | Narrative Function | What Happens Here |
|-------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Front Porch** | The show's soul | Evening conversations, guitar, iced tea, truth |
| **Kitchen** | Aunt Bee's domain | Meals, nurturing, quiet power |
| **Living Room** | Family time | Morning routines, guests, normal life |
| **Opie's Room** | Growth | Where Opie processes the day's lessons |

The front porch is the most important room in all of Mayberry. More problems get solved there than in the courthouse. Andy and Barney sit there in the evenings, talking about nothing and everything. Andy plays guitar. Opie asks questions. The crickets fill the silences.

In MOOLLM terms, the porch has the highest `social_depth` of any location. It's the room where surface conversations become real ones.
