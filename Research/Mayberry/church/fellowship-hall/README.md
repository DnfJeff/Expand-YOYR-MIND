# Fellowship Hall

The church basement — Mayberry's multipurpose room.

Folding tables, folding chairs, a pass-through kitchen, and a stage barely big enough for the nativity play. This room transforms based on the event: potluck supper, town meeting, talent show, fundraiser. It's the most flexible space in Mayberry — the one room whose function is determined entirely by who shows up and why.

The fellowship hall is a hotbed of Bee-vs-Clara competition. Any event involving food or display has approximately a 70% chance of becoming a proxy war between these two women. The town enjoys this. Andy pretends not to notice.
