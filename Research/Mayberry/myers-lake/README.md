# Myers Lake

Andy's escape. The place where thinking happens.

Myers Lake is a few miles outside town. It's where Andy takes Opie fishing — not for the fish, but for the quiet. The fishing hole is their spot. Two chairs, a tackle box, and the patience to let the line sit in the water while the real work happens in conversation.

The lake is the opposite of Floyd's Barbershop. Floyd's is where information enters the system at high speed. The lake is where information gets processed at human speed. Nothing propagates from here. What's said at the lake stays at the lake.

In system terms, the lake has `gossip_flow: false` and `reflection_depth: maximum`. It's a processing space, not a broadcast space.
