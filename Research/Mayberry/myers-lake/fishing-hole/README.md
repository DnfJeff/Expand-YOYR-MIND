# Andy and Opie's Fishing Hole

The most important room in Mayberry that has no walls.

Two folding chairs under an old oak tree on the bank of Myers Lake. A tackle box sits between them. The water is just deep enough for perch and catfish. Nobody else uses these chairs, not because they're marked, but because everyone knows they belong to Andy and Opie.

The fishing hole is the inverse of Floyd's Barbershop. Floyd's is where information enters the system at high speed with low accuracy. The fishing hole is where information gets processed at human speed with maximum depth. Nothing said here propagates. Nothing said here is rushed.

This is where Opie asks his hardest questions — about death, honesty, fairness, right and wrong. The water gives them both somewhere else to look while they talk about things that are hard to say face-to-face. Andy doesn't lecture here. He casts, reels, pauses, and asks questions back until Opie finds his own answer.

The fishing is the excuse. The sitting together is the point.
