# Howard Sprague

**County Clerk** — the most organized man in the least organized town.

Howard Sprague has filed every document in Mayberry's history in perfect alphabetical order. His desk is immaculate. His suits are pressed. His schedule is exact. He lives with his mother, who has opinions about everything Howard does, and Howard follows every one of them.

The comedy of Howard is the gap between who he is and who he wants to be. He's tidy when he wants to be spontaneous. He's cautious when he wants to be bold. He's at home with his mother when he wants to be out with the guys. He daydreams about adventure and then alphabetizes the daydream.

Howard is Mayberry's loneliest character. Not because people don't like him — they do, in the mild way you like someone who's always polite and never memorable. Howard's loneliness comes from self-suppression. He follows his mother's rules so thoroughly that he's lost track of his own preferences. His `adventure` need is marked `critical` in his CHARACTER.yml, but his `mother_approval` need overrides it every time.

The rare episodes where Howard breaks out — tries something new, goes somewhere unexpected — are the most bittersweet in the series. He always returns to his defaults. But for a moment, you saw who he might have been.

**The design insight:** Howard demonstrates **need conflict** — when two needs in a CHARACTER.yml contradict each other. His `adventure` need and his `mother_approval` need can't both be satisfied. The system must choose, and it always chooses the same way. That predictability IS the character. The sadness is the system working correctly.
