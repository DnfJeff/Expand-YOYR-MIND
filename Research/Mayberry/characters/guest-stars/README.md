# Guest Stars — Mayberry

Visitors to Mayberry are rare and significant. In a town where everybody knows everybody, a new face is an event. These guest stars bring the outside world into Mayberry — and Mayberry, being Mayberry, absorbs them, teaches them something, and sends them on their way.

## The Mayberry Visitor Pattern

Unlike the Danny Thomas Show's celebrity-visitor model, Mayberry's guests are ordinary people changed by contact with the town:

```
Stranger arrives → Town is curious → Andy observes
→ Problem surfaces → Andy addresses with patience, not force
→ Visitor learns something → Mayberry remains unchanged
→ Visitor leaves, carrying a piece of Mayberry with them
```

Mayberry doesn't change for visitors. Visitors change for Mayberry.
