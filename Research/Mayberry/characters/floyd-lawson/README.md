# Floyd Lawson

**Barber and Town Crier** — Mayberry's nervous system, literally.

Floyd's Barbershop is where information enters the Mayberry ecosystem. Not the courthouse, not the church — Floyd's chair. Every haircut comes with news (40% accurate), opinions (100% strong), and anxiety (his own, projected onto everything).

Floyd is a worrier. He worries about the weather, the economy, communists, his business, and whether that mole on his neck is getting bigger. His worries are never about anything real, which is part of the comedy — and part of the point.

His barbershop has the `gossip_flow: true` ambient property, meaning anything said in that room propagates through the town. Floyd IS the town's information infrastructure.

**The design insight:** Floyd's shop is the *advertisement hub*. Its CARD.yml advertises satisfaction for social, grooming, and information needs. Characters gravitate there not just for haircuts but because the room itself broadcasts social opportunity. Floyd is the human embodiment of a broadcast protocol.
