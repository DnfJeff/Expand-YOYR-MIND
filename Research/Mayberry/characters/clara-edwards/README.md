# Clara Edwards

**Neighbor, Church Lady, and Bee's Worthy Opponent**

Clara Edwards lives next door to the Taylors and is Aunt Bee's closest friend in Mayberry. They share recipes, attend church together, sit on committees together, and compete with each other in a rivalry so deep and so constant that it has become the foundation of their friendship rather than a threat to it.

Clara's pickles won the county fair last year. Bee has not recovered. Bee may never recover. The pickle contest is the Cold War of Mayberry — a conflict that defines everything without ever escalating to actual hostility. Clara knows what those pickles mean to Bee. Bee knows that Clara knows. They will never discuss it directly. They will discuss it indirectly in every conversation they have for the rest of their lives.

Outside the competition, Clara is a pillar of community life — church functions, garden clubs, town committees. She's traditional, opinionated, and convinced that standards are slipping. She means well. She means well *competitively*.

**The design insight:** Clara is the **frenemy pattern** — a character whose relationship with another character is built on simultaneous love and rivalry. Her CHARACTER.yml has a `decision_making: measure_against_bee` behavioral pattern, meaning her autonomous choices are filtered through comparison with Aunt Bee. Two characters linked this way generate conflict without requiring external stimulus. The system feeds itself.
