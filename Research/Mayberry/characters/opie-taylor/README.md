# Opie Taylor

**Andy's son** — the show's moral compass in training.

Opie is a kid learning how the world works. He makes mistakes — kills a bird with his slingshot, keeps a found wallet, lies about a fight. Every time, Andy doesn't punish him. Andy sets up a situation where Opie discovers for himself what the right thing is.

Opie is the audience's proxy. When he learns something, we learn it. When he struggles with a moral question, the show is asking us to struggle with it too. He's curious, honest (eventually), and braver than he knows.

He lives for fishing at the lake with his Pa, playing with his friends, and being taken seriously by the adults.

**The design insight:** Opie represents the _Play-Learn-Lift cycle_. He plays (fishing, exploring), learns (moral lessons through experience), and lifts (internalizes the principles). He's Papert's constructionism in a straw hat.
