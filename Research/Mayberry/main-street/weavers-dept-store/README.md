# Weaver's Department Store

Mayberry's general store — bolts of fabric, bins of hardware, racks of sensible clothing.

Mrs. Weaver runs the cash register and knows everyone's account balance from memory. Nobody uses checks at Weaver's. She writes it in the book. The honor system works in Mayberry because the social cost of defaulting on your account at Weaver's exceeds any possible financial benefit.

The wooden floor creaks in specific spots that the regulars have memorized. The store carries everything Mayberry needs and nothing it doesn't. If Weaver's doesn't stock it, you probably don't need it — or you drive to Mt. Pilot, which is its own kind of statement.
