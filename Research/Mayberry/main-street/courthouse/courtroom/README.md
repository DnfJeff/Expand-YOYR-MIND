# Courtroom

The room Mayberry rarely needs.

A formal space upstairs — dark wood benches, a judge's bench, the town seal on the wall. Dust motes drift through tall windows. When cases DO come here, it's an event. The whole town shows up, sits in the gallery, and treats it as theater — because in Mayberry, a courtroom proceeding is so rare that it becomes a spectacle.

Most of what would go to court in other towns gets resolved downstairs in Andy's office, or on someone's porch, or at Floyd's over a haircut. The courtroom exists as a reminder that formal systems are available. Mayberry just prefers the informal ones.
