# Wally's Filling Station

Gas, oil, and the Pyle cousins — Mayberry's mechanical heart.

Two gas pumps out front, a garage bay around the side. Wally owns the place but is rarely seen — Gomer and Goober run the day-to-day. Full service means full service: Gomer tops off the tank, checks the oil, the tires, the water, cleans the windshield, and tells you about his day. Whether you asked or not.

The [garage bay](garage-bay/) is where Goober transforms from a simple, crowd-following good ol' boy into a mechanical genius. Give him a broken engine and he's a savant. The room determines which Goober you get — a clear example of how location activates different aspects of a character's behavioral model.

The station doubles as a hangout for the younger crowd and anyone who likes leaning against things while talking.
