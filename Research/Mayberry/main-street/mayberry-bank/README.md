# Mayberry Bank

The one place in Mayberry where the relaxed vibe breaks.

Marble floors, brass fixtures, a teller cage with frosted glass, and a pendulum clock that ticks loud enough to remind you time is money. The bank manager's office has a closed door — the only closed door in Mayberry — which makes people nervous just by existing.

Money makes Mayberry uncomfortable. The town runs on handshakes, trust, and store credit. The bank represents the formal financial system that exists underneath the informal one, and nobody likes being reminded of it. The temperature is always cold. The mood is always formal. The clock is always ticking.

In system terms, the bank is an `anxiety_generator` — a room whose ambient properties actively shift the tone away from Mayberry's default `gentle_humor`. Characters behave slightly differently here: more careful, more quiet, more aware that this room operates on different rules.
