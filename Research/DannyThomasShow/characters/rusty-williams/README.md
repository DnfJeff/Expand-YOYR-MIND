# Rusty Williams

**Danny's Mirror** — the son who inherited everything his father can't control.

Rusty is ten years old and already has Danny's timing, Danny's volume, and Danny's instinct for turning any situation into a performance. He's a smart-mouthed, quick-witted kid who tests every boundary to find out which ones are real. He pushes back, talks back, and talks fast — and Danny recognizes every single move because he invented them.

The relationship between Danny and Rusty is the show's deepest recurring tension. When Danny yells at Rusty, he's yelling at a younger version of himself. When Rusty pushes Danny's buttons, he's using tools he inherited without instruction. They clash because they're identical, and neither can see the resemblance clearly enough to laugh about it. Kathy sees it. Kathy always sees it.

Rusty uses humor the way Andy Taylor uses silence — as a processing tool, a defense mechanism, and a way of testing the world. When Rusty cracks a joke about something, he's actually asking a question. Danny sometimes catches this. When he does, the show achieves its best moments.

**The design insight:** Rusty is the **inheritance-in-action** pattern. His CHARACTER.yml traits are a subset of Danny's, slightly remixed. The system can generate Rusty's behavior by running a younger, less filtered version of Danny's behavioral model. He's a character defined by what he inherited and what he hasn't learned to control yet.
