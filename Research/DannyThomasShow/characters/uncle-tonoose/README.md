# Uncle Tonoose

**The Force of Nature. The Uninvited. The Unstoppable.**

Uncle Tonoose doesn't visit — he *arrives*. He materializes at the apartment door with luggage, opinions, and a family mandate that overrides whatever plans Danny had. His voice fills every room. His personality fills every room. His *gestures* fill every room.

Tonoose is Danny's Lebanese uncle from the old country tradition. He's the family patriarch who dispenses wisdom whether it was requested or not. He arranges marriages. He critiques Danny's career. He takes over the kitchen. He rearranges the furniture. He stays longer than anyone wants and shorter than he thinks.

He is pure love expressed as chaos. Every action comes from genuine care for his family. The delivery just happens to include shouting, crying, theatrical exits, triumphant returns, obscure proverbs, and deeply questionable matchmaking.

**The design insight:** Uncle Tonoose is the ultimate *visitor pattern* — he's the quintessential disruption that the system must accommodate. His CHARACTER.yml has `visitor_impact: seismic`, meaning his presence overrides the ambient tone of any room he enters. He doesn't inherit from WORLD.yml — he overwrites it.
