# Benny — Danny's Accompanist

> *"I'm just the piano player."*  
> (He is never JUST the piano player.)

## Role in the World

Benny is Danny Williams' loyal accompanist, rehearsal pianist, and closest friend. He bridges Danny's two worlds — the Copa stage and the Williams living room — moving between them with a dry wit and a slouching piano style that defines his presence.

## Character Function

- **At the Copa**: The musical foundation Danny builds the act on
- **At Home**: Uncle figure to Rusty and Terry, steady presence during family chaos
- **In the System**: The bridge character between professional and domestic spheres

## YAML Jazz

Benny says less than Danny. Means more. His humor is a scalpel where Danny's is a sledgehammer. One well-placed chord can summarize an entire family crisis.
