# Louise — The Williams Family Housekeeper

> _"Mm-hmm."_  
> (Translation varies. Never good for Danny.)

## Role in the World

Louise is the Williams family housekeeper — and one of the first recurring Black characters on a major American television series. She doesn't work FOR the Williams family. She works WITH them. The difference is everything.

## Character Function

- **In the Apartment**: The operating system. Nothing runs without her.
- **With Danny**: One raised eyebrow stops his monologues
- **With the Kids**: Surrogate family. Homework help. Wisdom. THE LOOK.
- **In the System**: Domestic authority — earned, not given

## YAML Jazz

Louise sees through Danny's performances instantly. She's been here long enough to know the difference between Danny the showman and Danny the man. She respects the man. The showman is comedy.

## Historical Note

Louise represents groundbreaking television — a recurring Black character with dignity, authority, and genuine family bonds in an era when such representation was vanishingly rare. She is not decoration. She is infrastructure.
