# New York Streets

**The bustling city we rarely see but always feel.**

The Danny Thomas Show didn't film on New York streets — the city was mostly implied through window views, arrival stories, and the constant reference to places Danny had to be. But the city is a character. Its energy seeps into every scene. The apartment has street noise. Danny takes the subway. Visitors arrive from other boroughs, other states, other countries.

These locations are **Tardis directories** — they're sparse in files but represent enormous conceptual space. The LLM expands them as the narrative demands. A story about Danny getting stuck in traffic opens up `theater-district/` with detail. A story about the family in the park opens up `central-park/`. The folders exist as seeds.

## The City as Contrast

New York serves the show the way Mt. Pilot serves Mayberry — as the wider world that provides contrast and pressure. But where Mt. Pilot is a boundary (the edge of Mayberry's gentle influence), New York is an ocean (the chaos that the apartment floats in).

| Space | Function |
|-------|----------|
| [Theater District](theater-district/) | Danny's professional world — Copa, agents, other clubs |
| [Central Park](central-park/) | The family's outdoor space — the closest thing to Mayberry |
| [Neighborhood](neighborhood/) | The everyday — delis, dry cleaners, the walk to the subway |
