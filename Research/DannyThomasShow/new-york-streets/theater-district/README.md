# Theater District

The professional ecosystem Danny swims in — Broadway marquees, agents' offices, and restaurants where deals happen over lunch.

This is a **Tardis directory** — sparse by default, expandable when an episode needs it. A casting office materializes when Danny auditions. A rival club appears when competition drives the plot. A theater lobby fills with opening-night tension for exactly one episode, then folds back into the directory's ambient hum.

The theater district represents Danny's professional identity beyond the Copa. It's where he runs into other entertainers, confronts professional jealousy, and is reminded that show business is bigger than his corner of it. The emotional register is competitive in a way the Copa isn't — the Copa is Danny's turf, but the theater district belongs to everyone.

Characters encountered here are typically **one-episode visitors** who bring professional disruption: a comedian who's getting better reviews, a producer with an offer, an old colleague who needs a favor.
