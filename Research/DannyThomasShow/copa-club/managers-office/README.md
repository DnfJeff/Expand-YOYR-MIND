# Copa Club — Manager's Office

Where entertainment meets commerce, and Danny loses arguments about money.

A cluttered desk, phone with two lines, a calendar marked with booking dates, and framed photos of every act that's played the Copa. Phil Brokaw sits here with a cigar and a contract. Danny sits across from him with objections and no leverage.

The manager's office is the show's **business layer** — the acknowledgment that Danny's art happens inside a commercial system. Phil books the acts. Phil negotiates the fees. Phil delivers bad news with a shrug and good news with the same shrug. Danny's relationship with this room is adversarial in tone but functional in practice.

In MOOLLM terms, this room demonstrates **role inversion through context**. In every other room, Danny is the dominant personality. Here, Phil has positional authority. The room's defaults — `formality: professional`, `tone: transactional` — override Danny's natural `entertainer` mode and force him into `negotiator` mode, which he's not particularly good at.
