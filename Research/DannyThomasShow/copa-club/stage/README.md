# Copa Club — Stage

Where Danny Williams becomes Danny Williams.

A spotlight, a microphone on a stand, and darkness where the audience sits. This is the simplest room in the entire system and the most powerful. The stage strips away everything except Danny and the material. No family, no visitors, no uncle yelling from the living room. Just the act.

Danny's `stage_mode` activates fully here: timing tightens, voice projects, the self-deprecating humor that plays as vulnerability at home plays as craft on stage. Same words, different room, different character rendering. The room determines the performance.

The stage has a single CARD.yml advertisement: `PERFORM`. It satisfies Danny's deepest need — `recognition` — while simultaneously draining `energy` and `comfort`. The trade-off is the engine of his character: he needs the stage the way Otis Campbell needs the jail cell, except Danny would never admit it.
