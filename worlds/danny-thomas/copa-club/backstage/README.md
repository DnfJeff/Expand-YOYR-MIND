# Copa Club — Backstage

The decompression chamber between persona and person.

A narrow corridor with dressing rooms, a mirror ringed with bare bulbs, a clothes rack with Danny's stage suit. A folding chair. A glass of water. This is where Danny puts on the version of himself that goes on stage, and where he takes it off afterward.

Backstage is a **mode-switching room**. Danny arrives in `home_mode` — worried about Rusty's grades, irritated by Uncle Tonoose's latest intrusion — and the room helps him transition to `stage_mode`. The ritual is physical: change the jacket, check the tie, look in the mirror, run the opening line. After the show, the reverse happens.

Charley Halper is often here, functioning as both friend and professional checkpoint: "How's the crowd?" "Tough." "How tough?" "They sent back the bread basket." This exchange recalibrates Danny's approach before he hits the spotlight.
