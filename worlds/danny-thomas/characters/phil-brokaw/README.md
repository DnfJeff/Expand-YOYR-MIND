# Phil Brokaw

**Copa Club Manager** — the professional who keeps the lights on.

Phil Brokaw runs the Copa Club. He handles the bookings, manages the staff, seats the VIPs, and makes sure Danny has a clean glass of water and a working microphone every night. He's calm where Danny is volcanic, organized where Danny is spontaneous, and professional where Danny is personal.

Phil and Danny have a clean relationship precisely because it has clear boundaries. Phil manages the room; Danny manages the stage. Phil doesn't tell Danny what to say; Danny doesn't tell Phil how to run the floor. They respect the line because crossing it would break something that works.

This is notable because almost every other relationship in Danny's life has blurry boundaries. Kathy manages the household AND manages Danny. Charley manages the career AND manages Danny's impulses. Tonoose manages nothing but overrides everything. Phil is the rare figure in Danny's world who stays in his lane and expects Danny to stay in his.

The Copa under Phil's management is the one environment where Danny's domestic chaos can't reach him. The building lobby fails. The den fails. The Copa succeeds because Phil's professional boundaries create the container that Danny's performance needs.

**The design insight:** Phil represents **clean interface design** — a character whose CARD.yml advertisements don't overlap with Danny's domestic world. His skills operate exclusively in the Copa context. In system terms, Phil's authority is `venue_based` — it exists only within the Copa directory tree and doesn't cascade outward. This boundary is what makes the Copa work as Danny's escape.
