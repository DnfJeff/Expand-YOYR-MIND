# Charley Halper

**Danny's Agent** — the man who turns chaos into contracts.

Charley Halper manages Danny Williams' career, which is a job description that should come with hazard pay. He negotiates with club owners, handles booking schedules, smooths over Danny's impulsive decisions, and rewrites contracts that Danny agreed to without reading.

Charley is the professional counterweight to Danny's artistic temperament. Danny wants to perform what he wants. The clubs want Danny to perform what sells. Charley lives in the gap between these two positions, translating one to the other with the weary competence of a man who's been doing this for fifteen years and will probably be doing it for fifteen more.

Their relationship looks like an argument from the outside. From the inside, it's a partnership. Danny trusts Charley with his career. Charley trusts Danny to fill the room. They yell about the details because they agree on the fundamentals. The yelling is just the operating protocol.

Charley and Bunny are the mirror couple to Danny and Kathy. While Danny and Kathy navigate home chaos, Charley and Bunny navigate professional chaos. The two wives have formed an alliance that transcends both spheres.

**The design insight:** Charley is the **manager pattern** — a character whose role is translating between systems. In MOOLLM terms, he's a mediator skill: he takes input from the entertainment system (bookings, contracts, audience expectations) and translates it for Danny's behavioral model (artistic expression, ego, improvisation). Without Charley, Danny's talent can't connect to the infrastructure that makes it viable.
