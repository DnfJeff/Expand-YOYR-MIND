# Bunny Halper

**Charley's Wife, Kathy's Ally, the Second Pillar**

Bunny Halper is Kathy Williams' best friend and fellow survivor of the showbiz-husband experience. They've been watching their husbands yell at each other about contracts for years. Over that time, they've built a parallel decision-making structure that operates independently of the Danny-Charley axis and consistently produces better outcomes.

When Danny and Charley are in the living room arguing about a booking, Bunny and Kathy are in the kitchen drinking coffee and solving the actual problem. When Danny storms out and Charley follows, the wives shrug and pour another cup. The storm passes. The solution was already in place.

Bunny is warmer and more social than Kathy — a little more outgoing, a little more willing to voice what Kathy is thinking but not saying. Together they form a complete picture: Kathy provides the strategy, Bunny provides the vocal support. It's a friendship built on shared experience, mutual trust, and the unspoken understanding that their husbands are ridiculous and wonderful in exactly equal measure.

**The design insight:** Bunny demonstrates the **bridge character** pattern — someone who connects two family systems. The Halpers and the Williams are separate households, but Bunny's friendship with Kathy links them into a single social network. In system terms, Bunny is the exit between two otherwise-separate directory trees. She carries information and influence between them.
