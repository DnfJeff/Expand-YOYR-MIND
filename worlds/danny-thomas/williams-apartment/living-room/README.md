# Williams Apartment — Living Room

The stage where every domestic comedy plays out.

A sofa faces the front door — the classic sitcom arrangement where the audience sees who enters before anyone inside does. End tables with lamps, a coffee table with Danny's Variety stacked on top of old New Yorkers, and the phone on a side table that rings with either opportunity or disaster, never anything in between.

This is the room where the family gathers, where visitors are received, where Danny performs his bit about whatever happened at the Copa, and where Kathy delivers the quiet line that restructures the entire scene. It's the apartment's public face — open, warm, presentable.

The living room has `gossip_flow: false` because Williams family business stays in the Williams family. What comes through the front door (visitors, problems, Uncle Tonoose) is processed and dealt with here. What leaves through the front door is a resolved version of the story.
