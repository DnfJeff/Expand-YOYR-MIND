# Kids' Rooms

The part of the apartment the audience sees least, which is the point.

Rusty's room has baseball pennants, a model airplane hanging from the ceiling by fishing line, and homework that's perpetually half-finished. Terry's area (when she's visiting from college) is tidier — books, a small vanity, a suitcase that never fully gets unpacked because she's always between worlds.

The kids' rooms exist as **narrative holding areas**. Kids go here when the adult story needs to happen in the living room. They emerge when the plot needs them. This rhythmic appearance/disappearance is how the show manages ensemble size — the kids are present enough to matter but absent enough to give Danny and Kathy room to carry the episode.

In MOOLLM terms, this is a **low-advertisement room**. It offers `REST` and `STUDY` and not much else. Characters drift here by need, not by attraction. The system rarely routes anyone to the kids' rooms unless sleep or homework is involved.
