# Williams Apartment — Kitchen

Kathy's operational center and the family's real gathering point.

A small New York kitchen — barely room for two people to stand without bumping elbows. Yellow walls, a checkered floor, a window over the sink that looks at another building's window four feet away. The radio sits on the counter playing music that nobody listens to but everyone would notice if it stopped.

The kitchen is where the real conversations happen. The living room is for company; the kitchen is for family. Danny leans against the counter. Kathy cooks. They talk about what's actually going on — the stuff they edit for public consumption in the living room.

In MOOLLM terms, the kitchen `overrides` the apartment's default `formality: warm` with `formality: intimate`. Characters speak differently here. More honestly. Less performatively.
