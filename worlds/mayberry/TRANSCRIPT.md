## Episode 1: The Man in Goober's Hat

### Scene 1 — INT. ANDY'S OFFICE — MORNING (8:00 AM)

*The courthouse smells like coffee that's been on the burner too long. Andy sits at his desk, paper open to the fishing report. Barney stands at the window with binoculars he bought from a mail-order catalog.*

**BARNEY:** Andy. Andy. *Andy.*

**ANDY:** I hear you, Barn.

**BARNEY:** I have completed my morning threat assessment of Mayberry and surrounding environs.

*Andy takes a sip of coffee. Doesn't look up.*

**ANDY:** How many threats we got today?

**BARNEY:** Four. Possibly five, depending on how you classify the Henderson boy's slingshot.

**ANDY:** Same as yesterday?

**BARNEY:** There's one new development. Floyd called me at six-thirty this morning. Says he saw a stranger walking past his shop before dawn. *A stranger, Andy.* Walking. Before dawn.

**ANDY:** Walking.

**BARNEY:** Before *dawn.*

*Andy turns a page of his newspaper.*

**ANDY:** Could be somebody takin' a walk.

**BARNEY:** Or — and I want you to hear me out — it could be a casing operation. Floyd says the man was wearing a hat pulled down low. That's textbook, Andy. *Textbook.*

---

### Scene 2 — INT. TAYLOR KITCHEN — MORNING (8:15 AM)

*Aunt Bee stands at the counter, flour on her apron, staring at a row of mason jars like a general surveying a battlefield.*

*Clara Edwards appears at the back door. She knocks once and walks in, because in Mayberry the knock is a courtesy, not a request.*

**CLARA:** Morning, Bee. My, what are you doing with all those cucumbers?

**AUNT BEE:** *Not* pickles, if that's what you're implying, Clara.

**CLARA:** I wasn't implying anything. I was just saying, mine are already in the brine. Third day.

*Aunt Bee's jaw tightens exactly one-sixteenth of an inch.*

**AUNT BEE:** Well, I'm in no rush. A good pickle can't be rushed.

**CLARA:** No, of course not. *Some* pickles just know what they're doing faster than others.

*Aunt Bee reaches for the vinegar with unnecessary force.*

---

### Scene 3 — INT. SCHOOLHOUSE — MORNING (8:15 AM)

*Helen Crump writes "Long Division" on the chalkboard. Twenty-two children sit in various states of attention. Opie Taylor is attempting to trade his apple for Jimmy Tolliver's baseball card under his desk.*

**HELEN:** Opie Taylor.

*Opie freezes. The apple is halfway across the aisle.*

**OPIE:** Ma'am?

**HELEN:** Would you like to bring what you're holding up to the front of the class?

**OPIE:** ...It's just an apple, Miss Crump.

**HELEN:** Then you won't mind putting it on my desk where apples traditionally go.

*Opie walks to the front. The baseball card stays with Jimmy. Opie sits back down, appleless and cardless. Long division continues.*

---

### Scene 4 — INT. FLOYD'S BARBERSHOP — MORNING (8:30 AM)

*Floyd is sweeping hair that isn't there yet. The barber pole turns outside. Goober Pyle walks in wearing a hat nobody has seen before — a brown fedora, slightly too large.*

**FLOYD:** Morning, Goober. What — what is *that* on your head?

**GOOBER:** New hat. Found it at the Raleigh surplus store. You like it?

**FLOYD:** It's... it's very... *Where did you say you got that?*

**GOOBER:** Raleigh. I drove up yesterday afternoon. Got back real late. Walked home from Gomer's this morning — my car's still at the station.

*Floyd goes very still. His scissors stop mid-air.*

**FLOYD:** Walked. This morning. *Before dawn?*

**GOOBER:** I reckon. Sun wasn't full up yet.

*Floyd drops his broom.*

**FLOYD:** Goober, you sit right there. Don't move. I gotta make a call.

---

### Scene 5 — INT. ANDY'S OFFICE — MORNING (8:45 AM)

*The phone rings. Barney lunges for it. Andy lets him.*

**BARNEY:** Mayberry Sheriff's Office, Deputy Fife speaking. ... Uh-huh. ... Uh-*huh.* ... You don't say. ... YOU DON'T SAY.

*Barney hangs up. His face is the color of a man who has been proven right about everything.*

**BARNEY:** That was Floyd. The stranger? He's at Floyd's. Right now. Sitting in the waiting chair. *Bold as brass, Andy.*

**ANDY:** Did Floyd say who it was?

**BARNEY:** He said the man is wearing the same hat, pulled low. Andy, I am proceeding to Floyd's Barbershop to conduct a field investigation. I am requesting backup.

**ANDY:** Barn —

**BARNEY:** This is *exactly* what I trained for.

*Barney puts on his hat, checks that his bullet is in his shirt pocket, and walks out with the stride of a man who has watched too many FBI documentaries.*

*Andy sighs. Puts down his coffee. Follows.*

---

### Scene 6 — INT. FLOYD'S BARBERSHOP — MORNING (9:00 AM)

*Goober is sitting in the waiting chair, reading a magazine upside down. He hasn't noticed. Floyd is pressed against the back wall, holding his scissors like a weapon.*

*The door bursts open. Barney enters in a half-crouch.*

**BARNEY:** FREEZE! Nobody move! Deputy Fife, Mayberry Sheriff's Department!

*Goober looks up from his magazine.*

**GOOBER:** Hey, Barney.

*Barney freezes. His hand is on his holster.*

**BARNEY:** ...Goober?

**GOOBER:** You want a magazine? This one's got a real good article about transmissions. I think. The pictures are kinda confusing.

*Barney straightens up slowly. Floyd peeks out from behind his barber chair.*

**FLOYD:** That's — that's the stranger, Barney. The one I saw this morning.

**BARNEY:** Floyd. That's *Goober.*

**FLOYD:** Well he didn't look like Goober! He was wearing that — that *hat!*

*Andy appears in the doorway, leaning against the frame.*

**ANDY:** Morning, Goober.

**GOOBER:** Hey, Andy! You like my new hat?

**ANDY:** It's a fine hat, Goob.

---

### Scene 7 — INT. FLOYD'S BARBERSHOP — MORNING (9:15 AM)

*Barney is pacing. Floyd is cutting Goober's hair. Andy is sitting in the waiting chair with his arms crossed, wearing the expression of a man who is not going to say "I told you so" because he doesn't have to.*

**BARNEY:** In my defense, Floyd's description was consistent with a suspicious individual.

**ANDY:** Floyd said he saw a man walking.

**BARNEY:** Walking *suspiciously.*

**ANDY:** In a hat.

**BARNEY:** A *suspicious* hat.

*Goober tilts his head, and Floyd corrects it gently.*

**GOOBER:** It ain't suspicious. I paid four dollars for this hat.

**BARNEY:** I'm just saying — if it *had* been a criminal, we'd have had him. I had the barbershop surrounded. Well. The front, anyway.

**ANDY:** You did fine, Barn.

*Barney straightens his tie, mollified.*

**BARNEY:** I ran a tight operation.

**FLOYD:** Barney, you want me to take a little off the sides while you're here?

*Barney touches his hair. Considers. Sits in the chair after Goober.*

**BARNEY:** Well. Long as I'm here.

---

### Scene 8 — INT. TAYLOR KITCHEN / EXT. FRONT PORCH — MORNING (9:45 AM)

*Aunt Bee pulls a jar from the back of the shelf — the emergency reserve, the ones she made last September that even she admits turned out right. She sets them on the counter next to Clara's.*

**AUNT BEE:** Clara, I want you to try something.

*Clara opens the jar. Takes a bite. Her face goes through seven expressions in four seconds.*

**CLARA:** ...That's very good, Bee.

**AUNT BEE:** I know.

*On the front porch, Otis Campbell walks past, freshly released from his own custody, hat on straight, dignity intact. He tips his hat to nobody in particular.*

*Down at the schoolhouse, a bell rings. Opie bursts through the door for morning recess, the long division already forgotten, a whole playground in front of him.*

*At Floyd's, Barney sits in the barber chair telling Floyd about the time he single-handedly prevented a crime wave — which was this morning — while Floyd cuts his hair and nods and files the story away for the next customer.*

*And Andy Taylor walks back to the courthouse, takes his coffee off the burner, sits down, and turns to the crossword.*

*Morning in Mayberry. Everything exactly as it should be.*

## Episode 2: The Ancient Art

### Scene 1 — INT. ANDY'S OFFICE — LATE MORNING (11:00 AM)

*The typewriter has stopped. Barney holds up two pages, single-spaced despite his earlier intentions, and reads aloud.*

**BARNEY:** "Proposal for Physical Readiness Program, Mayberry Sheriff's Department. Submitted by Deputy Bernard P. Fife. Section One: The Threat Landscape."

**ANDY:** Barn, you got a threat landscape in there?

**BARNEY:** I got three sections, Andy. Threat landscape, methodology — that's the sumo part — and projected outcomes. I even put in a budget request. We need mats.

**ANDY:** How much are mats?

**BARNEY:** I don't know. I put "to be determined." That's how the government does it, Andy. You get approval first, *then* you figure out what it costs.

*Andy takes a slow sip of coffee.*

**ANDY:** And you want to show this to Mayor Stoner?

**BARNEY:** I want to *present* it. Formally. In his office. Man to man.

*Barney tucks the pages into a manila folder. He pats his hair. He checks his badge. He walks toward the door with the stride of a man who has never once been laughed at by a politician.*

---

### Scene 2 — INT. COURTHOUSE HALLWAY / MAYOR'S OFFICE — LATE MORNING (11:15 AM)

*Barney knocks on the mayor's door. Three crisp knocks, regulation spacing.*

**MAYOR STONER:** Come in.

*Barney enters. Mayor Stoner is at his desk, reading the Raleigh News. He doesn't look up.*

**BARNEY:** Mayor Stoner, Deputy Fife. I have a proposal for your consideration regarding departmental readiness.

**MAYOR STONER:** Mm-hmm.

**BARNEY:** It's — it's a physical fitness initiative. Based on an ancient Eastern combat discipline. Sumo.

*The mayor looks up. His eyebrows do something complicated.*

**MAYOR STONER:** ...Sumo.

**BARNEY:** Sumo. The art of the grand champions. Now, I know what you're thinking —

**MAYOR STONER:** I'm thinking those are the big fellas in the diapers.

**BARNEY:** They're not *diapers*, they're *mawashi*. They're traditional —

**MAYOR STONER:** Deputy, are you proposing that the Mayberry Sheriff's Department adopt the fighting style of three-hundred-pound men in their underpants?

**BARNEY:** It's not about the underpants, it's about the *leverage* —

**MAYOR STONER:** Because I can tell you right now what the Raleigh News would do with THAT.

*Barney's mouth opens and closes twice. The folder in his hands is shaking slightly.*

**MAYOR STONER:** Son, I appreciate your enthusiasm. I truly do. But the last thing this office needs is a photograph of a deputy in a sumo getup in front of the courthouse. Now if you'll excuse me.

*He goes back to his paper. Barney stands for three full seconds, then walks out. His back is very straight. His jaw is very tight.*

---

### Scene 3 — INT. ANDY'S OFFICE — NOON (12:00 PM)

*Andy is at his desk. The door opens and closes harder than it needs to.*

*Barney sits at his desk. He takes his hat off. He puts his hat on again. He organizes three pencils that don't need organizing.*

**ANDY:** How'd it go?

**BARNEY:** Fine. It went fine.

**ANDY:** ...

**BARNEY:** He called it diaper wrestling.

**ANDY:** He didn't.

**BARNEY:** He did. He said "three-hundred-pound men in their underpants." To *me.* A deputy of this department. A man who has *studied* judo, karate, and now the ancient art of sumo, and the Mayor of Mayberry called it diaper wrestling.

*Barney's hands are flat on the desk.*

**BARNEY:** And I want you to do something about it.

**ANDY:** Do something.

**BARNEY:** Talk to him. You're the sheriff. He listens to you. He doesn't listen to me — he doesn't even *see* me, Andy. I walked in there and he didn't look up for thirty seconds. Thirty seconds! I counted!

**ANDY:** Barn, what exactly do you want me to say to the mayor?

**BARNEY:** I want you to tell him that his deputy submitted a formal proposal through proper channels and it deserves a proper hearing. Not a joke. A *hearing.*

*Andy's quiet for a moment. He nods, once.*

**ANDY:** I'll talk to him.

**BARNEY:** ...You will?

**ANDY:** I said I would.

---

### Scene 4 — INT. MAYOR'S OFFICE — EARLY AFTERNOON (12:30 PM)

*Andy walks in without knocking.*

**MAYOR STONER:** Andy, I was just going to call you. I've got the county commissioner coming through Thursday for a surprise inspection —

**ANDY:** It's not a surprise if you know about it.

**MAYOR STONER:** The point is, I need the courthouse looking sharp. The department looking *professional.*

**ANDY:** Speaking of professional. Barney came to see you this morning.

**MAYOR STONER:** Your deputy? Something about sumo.

**ANDY:** He put a lot of work into that proposal, Mayor.

**MAYOR STONER:** Andy, you can't seriously —

**ANDY:** I'm not saying adopt sumo. I'm saying the man walked in here with a typed proposal, a cover letter, and a budget request, and you didn't read past the title.

*The mayor shifts in his chair.*

**ANDY:** You've got that county commissioner coming Thursday. How about instead of sumo, you let Barney put together a departmental readiness demonstration? Regulation stuff. Procedure, equipment, preparedness. Let him show the commissioner what Mayberry's department looks like.

**MAYOR STONER:** ...That's actually not bad. The commissioner sees a well-run department, reflects well on my administration...

**ANDY:** And Barney gets his hearing.

**MAYOR STONER:** Fine. Twenty minutes on Thursday. And Andy — no sumo.

**ANDY:** I'll handle it.

---

### Scene 5 — INT. FLOYD'S BARBERSHOP — AFTERNOON (1:30 PM)

*Floyd is cutting the mayor's hair. Goober is in the waiting chair in his new fedora.*

**FLOYD:** So the commissioner's coming Thursday?

**MAYOR STONER:** How do you know about that?

**GOOBER:** Barney told Gomer at the filling station and Gomer told me and I told Floyd.

**MAYOR STONER:** Does the WHOLE TOWN know?

**FLOYD:** Well... not *the whole town.* I haven't called Clara yet.

*The mayor stands up with the barber cape still on.*

**MAYOR STONER:** This is EXACTLY what I'm talking about! How does Mayberry expect to be taken seriously when every piece of official business is broadcast through a *barbershop?*

**FLOYD:** You want me to stop?

**MAYOR STONER:** ...Just take a little more off the sides.

---

### Scene 6 — INT. ANDY'S OFFICE — AFTERNOON (2:15 PM)

**ANDY:** The mayor wants a departmental readiness demonstration for the county commissioner on Thursday.

**BARNEY:** A demonstration?

**ANDY:** Twenty minutes. You put it together.

*Barney's eyes are getting wider.*

**BARNEY:** He wants *me* to put it together?

**ANDY:** He asked for you specifically.

*Barney is pacing. Already planning.*

**BARNEY:** I'll need a checklist. Equipment inspection. Radio procedure. Arrest protocol. Maybe a physical readiness component?

*He looks at the sumo book. Andy follows his gaze.*

**ANDY:** Regulation physical readiness, Barn.

*Barney opens his desk drawer and sets the sumo book inside, carefully, next to his whistle and his backup badge polish.*

**BARNEY:** Regulation.

*He closes the drawer.*

**BARNEY:** But Andy?

**ANDY:** Yeah, Barn?

**BARNEY:** It *is* a legitimate combat form.

**ANDY:** I know it is.

---

### Scene 7 — EXT. FRONT PORCH — EVENING (7:30 PM)

*The porch. The two rockers. The sky going from blue to orange. Barney has a clipboard.*

**BARNEY:** Okay, so I'm thinking we start with a walkthrough of the squad car. Show the equipment, the radio, the trunk inventory. Then inside — filing system, the new index tabs —

**ANDY:** The ones with the color codes?

**BARNEY:** Color-coded. Red for active, blue for closed, yellow for pending. The commissioner's going to lose his mind when he sees those tabs.

**ANDY:** What about after the filing system?

**BARNEY:** Arrest procedure demonstration. I'll need a volunteer.

**ANDY:** Don't arrest Gomer again.

**BARNEY:** I was going to arrest Goober. He holds still better.

*The streetlight comes on. A mockingbird starts up.*

**BARNEY:** Andy?

**ANDY:** Hmm?

**BARNEY:** Thanks. For talking to the mayor.

**ANDY:** Didn't do much.

**BARNEY:** You did enough.

*Andy picks up the guitar. Plays something slow — the sound the evening makes when it's working right. Barney goes back to his clipboard. He adds "PHYSICAL READINESS (regulation)" to the bottom, underlines it twice, and doesn't look at the desk drawer where the sumo book is.*

*But he thinks about it.*

---

### Scene 8 — INT. TAYLOR HOUSE — NIGHT (8:45 PM)

*Aunt Bee is putting dishes away. Opie comes downstairs in his pajamas.*

**OPIE:** Aunt Bee, what's sumo?

**AUNT BEE:** Where did you hear that word?

**OPIE:** Gomer told me Barney's learning sumo. He said Barney stomped all around the courthouse and knocked the phone off the hook.

**AUNT BEE:** If Barney's doing it, I'm sure it's very... energetic.

**OPIE:** Gomer says it's where big men in their underwear push each other around.

**AUNT BEE:** I think that's enough about that. Go brush your teeth.

*Opie goes upstairs. Aunt Bee looks out the window at Andy and Barney on the porch, shakes her head with the smallest smile, and turns off the kitchen light.*

*Outside, the guitar plays on. The evening does what Mayberry evenings do — goes slow, goes kind, goes to bed with the porch light still on.*

---

*END — Episode 2*

## Episode 3: The Checklist

### Scene 1 — EXT. FRONT PORCH — NIGHT (9:15 PM)

*The guitar has stopped. The mockingbird has ceded the night to crickets. Barney is still in the rocker, but the clipboard is changing. Lines being crossed out. New lines added. He holds the pencil like a surgeon holds a scalpel — too tight, too close to the tip.*

**BARNEY:** Andy, I have a question about the squad car inspection.

**ANDY:** Hmm.

**BARNEY:** Do I pop the trunk first or show the trunk inventory checklist first? Because if I show the checklist first, he can follow along. But if I open the trunk first, it has more *impact.*

**ANDY:** I think either way works fine, Barn.

**BARNEY:** But which way is *better?*

**ANDY:** Trunk first.

**BARNEY:** Trunk first. That's what I had.

*He writes "TRUNK FIRST" and underlines it. Then adds "(confirmed by Sheriff Taylor)" next to it.*

*Thelma Lou appears at the screen door.*

**THELMA LOU:** Barney, I brought cashew fudge. Are you two going to sit out here all night?

**BARNEY:** I'm working, Thelma Lou. The county commissioner's coming Thursday.

**THELMA LOU:** I know. Floyd told me. Floyd told everybody.

*She sets the fudge on the porch railing and sits on the step.*

**THELMA LOU:** What's my part?

**BARNEY:** Your part?

**THELMA LOU:** In the demonstration. I could type something. Or answer the phone. I've got a very professional phone voice.

*Something softens in Barney's face that has nothing to do with the fudge.*

**BARNEY:** ...I'll put you on the call-in. Page two, section four.

---

### Scene 2 — INT. TAYLOR HOUSE — NIGHT (10:00 PM)

*The house is closing down. Aunt Bee checks the back door, the stove, the windows.*

*Andy comes in from the porch, guitar under one arm.*

**AUNT BEE:** Barney gone home?

**ANDY:** Thelma Lou drove him. He's still working on the checklist in the car.

**AUNT BEE:** He's really excited about this commissioner thing.

**ANDY:** He is.

**AUNT BEE:** Andy, is this commissioner going to be... kind?

*Andy sets the guitar in the corner. He doesn't answer right away.*

**ANDY:** I don't know. But Barney'll be ready. He's always ready when it counts.

**AUNT BEE:** When it counts, yes. It's the rehearsals that worry me.

*She turns off the living room lamp. The house goes to sleep.*

---

### Scene 3 — EXT. MAIN STREET — NIGHT (10:30 PM)

*Mayberry at night is three streetlights, a thousand crickets, and Otis Campbell. He's walking toward the courthouse with the steady purpose of a man who knows exactly where he's going and exactly why.*

*He opens the courthouse door with his own key. Crosses to the jail cell. Hangs his hat on the hook. Lies down on the cot. Pulls the blanket up.*

*The cot creaks once. The courthouse clock ticks. Otis sleeps.*

---

### Scene 4 — INT. ANDY'S OFFICE — NEXT MORNING, WEDNESDAY (7:30 AM)

*A new day. The commissioner comes tomorrow. Andy's coffee is on. Barney arrives twelve minutes early — six minutes earlier than his usual six-minutes-early. He's carrying a folder, the clipboard, a stopwatch, and a hand-drawn floor plan of the courthouse.*

**ANDY:** Mornin', Barn.

**BARNEY:** Good morning. I've revised the checklist. We're down to seventeen-point-five minutes. That gives us a two-and-a-half minute buffer for questions.

**ANDY:** Seventeen and a half.

**BARNEY:** The trunk walkthrough is four minutes. Filing system, three. Radio demonstration, two. Arrest procedure, five. And physical readiness, three-and-a-half.

**ANDY:** You timed physical readiness?

**BARNEY:** Push-ups, sit-ups, and a regulation hundred-yard dash. I clocked the dash in my apartment hallway. Fourteen seconds.

**ANDY:** How long is your hallway?

**BARNEY:** ...It's not a hundred yards. But I extrapolated.

---

### Scene 5 — INT. ANDY'S OFFICE — MORNING (8:30 AM)

*Barney has commandeered the center of the office. Goober is standing where Barney told him to stand, wearing a slightly confused expression and his fedora.*

**BARNEY:** Okay, Goober. You're the suspect. When I say "freeze," you freeze. When I say "hands behind your back," you put your hands behind your back. When I say "you have the right to remain silent," you remain silent. Can you do that?

**GOOBER:** Sure, Barney.

*Barney walks to the door, turns, strides toward Goober.*

**BARNEY:** FREEZE!

*Goober freezes. Barney reaches for the handcuffs. They catch on his belt loop. He tugs. They don't come free. His belt shifts. He spins halfway around.*

**GOOBER:** Barney, you want me to help you with —

**BARNEY:** REMAIN SILENT!

*Andy reaches over, calmly unhoops the handcuffs from Barney's belt, and hands them to him.*

**BARNEY:** I knew they were there. I was testing the quick-draw.

*He cuffs Goober neatly. Goober examines the handcuffs with mechanical interest.*

**GOOBER:** These are Smith & Wesson Model 90s. Good action. Little stiff on the left ratchet though. You want me to oil that?

**BARNEY:** Goober. You are under arrest. You do not get to critique the equipment.

---

### Scene 6 — INT. FLOYD'S BARBERSHOP — MIDDAY (12:00 PM)

*Floyd is sweeping. Andy walks in. The shop is empty — lunch hour, everybody at the diner.*

**FLOYD:** Andy, is Barney all right?

**ANDY:** What do you mean?

**FLOYD:** He came in this morning and measured the distance from my front door to the barber chair. Sixteen feet. Then he wrote it down and left.

**ANDY:** He's preparing for the commissioner.

**FLOYD:** He measured my shop for the commissioner?

**ANDY:** I think he measured everything on Main Street, Floyd.

*Andy sits in the barber chair. Not for a haircut — just to sit.*

**ANDY:** Floyd, you remember when Barney studied judo?

**FLOYD:** He threw himself into the wall.

**ANDY:** And when he took up karate?

**FLOYD:** He broke the pencil sharpener.

**ANDY:** He's like that. He throws himself at things. Sometimes he bounces off. But he shows up. Every time. And tomorrow, Barney's going to be ready. More ready than anybody in Raleigh would believe.

**FLOYD:** ...You think so?

**ANDY:** I *know* so.

**FLOYD:** You want me to give him a free haircut before tomorrow? Clean him up?

**ANDY:** That'd be real nice, Floyd.

---

### Scene 7 — INT. ANDY'S OFFICE — AFTERNOON (3:00 PM)

*Fourth rehearsal. Goober has been arrested, booked, and released three times.*

**BARNEY:** That's it. Sixteen-forty.

**GOOBER:** Can I take the handcuffs off now?

**BARNEY:** Right. Sorry, Goob.

*Gomer walks in with a brown paper bag.*

**GOMER:** Hey, Barney! Hey, Andy! Hey, Goob! I brought sandwiches from the diner! Tuna on white for everybody!

*He sets the bag directly on Barney's floor plan.*

**BARNEY:** Gomer! That's the — never mind. Thank you, Gomer.

**GOMER:** Are you still practicing the arresting? I can be the criminal this time. I've been working on my suspicious face.

*Gomer narrows his eyes and looks from side to side. He looks like a man who has misplaced his car keys.*

**ANDY:** That's very suspicious, Gomer.

**GOMER:** Thank you!

*They eat sandwiches. Barney reviews the checklist between bites. Andy watches him — the way Barney's hand doesn't shake, the way his voice is steady when he talks through procedure, the way a man who everybody thinks can't do anything is getting ready to do something, and getting it right.*

---

### Scene 8 — EXT. MAIN STREET — EVENING (6:00 PM)

*The afternoon light goes long and golden on Main Street. Barney walks out of Floyd's with a fresh haircut — Floyd's best work, straight razor on the neck, hot towel, the works.*

*He passes the courthouse and looks up at it. Tomorrow, inside those walls, he gets his twenty minutes.*

*At the filling station, Gomer is closing up. He waves. Goober's under a hood, the fedora sitting on the fender.*

*At the Taylor house, Aunt Bee is setting the table. Opie throws a baseball at nothing in the yard.*

*Barney walks to the squad car. Opens the trunk. Checks the inventory one more time: flares, first aid kit, blanket, spare tire, lug wrench, flashlight, batteries. Everything where it should be. He closes the trunk. It latches clean.*

*He takes the checklist from his pocket. Reads it once more. Folds it. Puts it in his breast pocket, next to the bullet.*

*Tomorrow.*

*The streetlights come on, one by one. Mayberry closes its eyes. And in the courthouse, Otis lets himself in, hangs up his hat, and the cot creaks the same way it always does.*

---

*END — Episode 3*
