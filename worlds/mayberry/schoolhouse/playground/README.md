# School Playground

Where the real education happens when the bell rings.

A dirt-and-grass yard with a swing set, monkey bars, and a baseball diamond worn into the outfield. The boundaries are tradition, not fencing. This is where Opie and his friends negotiate friendships, settle disputes, and learn the social skills that the classroom can only name.

The playground is to the schoolhouse what Floyd's Barbershop is to the courthouse — the informal space where the real social dynamics operate. Helen Crump oversees recess the way Andy oversees Mayberry: present but not heavy-handed, stepping in only when someone's about to get hurt.
