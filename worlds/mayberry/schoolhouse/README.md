# Mayberry Schoolhouse

Helen Crump's domain — the one institution in Mayberry that Andy doesn't govern.

The schoolhouse is where Opie and his friends spend their days learning reading, writing, arithmetic, and the social dynamics that will define their adult lives. Helen runs her classroom with competence and warmth. She expects respect and earns it. The children like her because she treats them as people, not as inconveniences.

The [playground](playground/) around the side is where the unofficial curriculum happens — alliances form, conflicts arise, and kids practice being people before Helen has to teach them the formal version.

In system terms, the schoolhouse is an **independent authority zone**. Helen's governance model doesn't inherit from Andy's. The badge has no jurisdiction here. This boundary is part of what makes their romantic relationship work — they're equals because they govern separate domains.
