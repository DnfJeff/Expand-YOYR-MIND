# The Front Porch

The soul of the show. More problems get solved here than in the courthouse.

Two rocking chairs, a porch swing, a side table for iced tea. Andy's guitar leans against the wall. This isn't a room — it's a philosophy made of wood and screen windows.

The front porch activates at dusk. Before that, it's furniture. After dark, it becomes the place where Mayberry's real conversations happen. Andy and Barney sit here after dinner, talking about nothing and everything. Andy plays guitar. Opie asks questions about something that happened at school. The crickets fill the silences, and the silences are where the meaning lives.

The porch has `gossip_flow: false` in its ambient properties — the opposite of Floyd's. What's said on the porch stays on the porch. This is the confessional, not the broadcast tower. It's the one place where Barney drops the bluster, where Andy drops the patience, where people say what they actually think.

In system terms, the porch has `social_depth: maximum`. Surface conversations become real ones here. The rocking chairs force a shared rhythm. The darkness provides cover. The guitar provides transition music between topics that are too heavy for direct approach.
