# Taylor Kitchen

Aunt Bee's sovereign territory.

The stove is always warm. Something is always in the oven. The cookie jar is on the counter, and Opie knows exactly how to open it quietly. This kitchen runs on Bee's schedule — breakfast at 7, dinner at 6 — and the Taylor house orbits this schedule whether anyone admits it or not.

Andy is Sheriff of Mayberry. Aunt Bee is Sovereign of this kitchen. These jurisdictions do not overlap. Andy knows better than to issue directives here. Bee manages the household infrastructure that makes everything else possible — Andy and Opie can go fishing because Bee holds down the fort.

When Bee enters a pickle contest or tries Chinese cooking, this kitchen becomes a laboratory. When Clara comes for coffee, it becomes a diplomatic chamber. When Opie sneaks cookies, it becomes a gentle courtroom. The room's function follows whoever's using it, but Bee's authority is always ambient.
