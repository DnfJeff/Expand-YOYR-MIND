# Opie's Room

Where a boy processes the world.

Model planes hang from the ceiling. The slingshot on the dresser is the one that taught him about consequences — the bird he killed and buried, the conversation with Pa that followed. A baseball glove on the bed. School books stacked unevenly. The piggy bank on the shelf holds the charity dollar he struggled over.

Every object in this room tells a story about a lesson learned. The room is a museum of Opie's moral development, curated by accident. He doesn't know that's what it is. Andy does.

This is where Opie goes to think after the hard conversations happen on the porch. The porch is where the question gets asked. This room is where the answer settles.
