# Taylor Living Room

The neutral zone — where daily life happens without drama.

A comfortable room with a sofa, an armchair, and a television set. Family photos on the mantle. Reader's Digest on the coffee table. This is the room where the Taylors start and end the day — breakfast routines, evening TV, guests being received.

The living room is less narratively charged than the front porch or the kitchen. It doesn't have the porch's depth or the kitchen's sovereignty. It's domestic middle ground — the place where nothing especially important happens, which is exactly what makes it feel like home. Not every room needs to be a crucible. Some rooms just need to be the place where you put your feet up.
