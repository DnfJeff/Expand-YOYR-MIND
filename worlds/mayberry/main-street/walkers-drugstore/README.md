# Walker's Drugstore

Soda fountain, pharmacy, and Mayberry's intergenerational meeting ground.

A long marble counter with spinning stools. Glass apothecary jars on the shelves. A jukebox in the corner. Teenagers sit at the counter after school. Adults come for medicine and stay for conversation. It's one of the few places in Mayberry where generations mix freely — kids and adults sharing counter space without awkwardness.

The drugstore has `gossip_flow: true` but higher accuracy than Floyd's (~55%) because the pharmacist tends to correct the worst misinformation. It's a secondary social hub — not as intense as Floyd's chair, but steadier traffic and broader age range.
