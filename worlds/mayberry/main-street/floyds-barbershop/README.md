# Floyd's Barbershop

**Mayberry's real nerve center.** More news gets exchanged here than at the newspaper. The accuracy rate hovers around 40%, but nobody's checking.

Floyd has one chair (the other hasn't been used since his partner left). He gives the same basic haircut to everyone but treats each one like a masterpiece. The real service isn't grooming — it's gossip, therapy, and community.

## Social Mechanics

Floyd's has `gossip_flow: true` in its ambient properties. This means:

- Anything said here propagates through Mayberry
- Accuracy degrades with each hop
- By evening, a simple comment about the weather becomes a tornado warning

This is the Sims advertisement system in action. Floyd's CARD.yml advertises satisfaction for: social, grooming, information, and comfort needs. Characters with low social needs gravitate here. Characters with information needs show up to listen. The room is a social magnet.
