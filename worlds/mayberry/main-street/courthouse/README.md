# The Mayberry Courthouse

Two-story brick building at the top of Main Street. Downstairs: Andy's office, the jail cell. Upstairs: courtroom (rarely used — Andy prefers the office). County records are filed and probably organized by Howard Sprague.

This is Andy's base of operations, but calling it a "police station" misses the point. It's more like a community center where a badge happens to live. People come here to report a stray dog, complain about a neighbor's fence, ask Andy to settle a bet, or just sit and talk.

The jail cell door is famously unlocked. Otis uses it as a bed-and-breakfast. The key is on a hook by the door — more symbolic than functional.
