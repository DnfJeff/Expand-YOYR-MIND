# Andy's Office

Where problems come to be solved through conversation, not law.

A wooden desk, a swivel chair, a gun rack Andy rarely touches, and a coffee maker that's always on. Two visitor chairs face the desk. That arrangement isn't an accident — Andy's office is designed for listening. The visitor faces Andy; Andy faces the visitor. Eye level. No barrier that matters.

This office barely functions as law enforcement. It functions as therapy, mediation, advice column, and town hall — all disguised as a sheriff's office. People come here to report a stray dog, complain about a neighbor's fence, ask Andy to settle a bet, or just sit and talk. The badge is on the desk. The real work happens in the chairs.

The CARD.yml here advertises `SETTLE_DISPUTE`, `REPORT_IN`, and `DRINK_COFFEE`. The coffee gets more use than the badge.
