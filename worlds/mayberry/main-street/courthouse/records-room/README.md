# Records Room

Howard Sprague's kingdom — the most organized space in an unorganized town.

Floor-to-ceiling filing cabinets in perfect formation. Every birth, death, deed, and dispute in Mayberry history filed in alphabetical order. Howard's desk is spotless. His typewriter ribbon is fresh. The room is kept at exactly 68 degrees — for document preservation, he says; for his comfort, if we're honest.

This room is the town's memory in physical form. Howard is its custodian. His dedication to order in this room contrasts with his inability to order his own life, which is either ironic or deeply human, depending on how you look at it.
