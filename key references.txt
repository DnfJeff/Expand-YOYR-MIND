## **Philosophy & Education (The Foundation)**

**Core Thinkers:**
1. **Seymour Papert** - Constructionism, Logo, "learn by building"
   - Book: *Mindstorms* (1980)
   - Concept: Microworlds, debugging as learning
   
2. **Alan Kay** - Smalltalk, "The computer revolution hasn't happened yet"
   - Dynabook vision, messaging, children programming
   - Look up: "Doing with Images Makes Symbols" talk
   
3. **Marvin Minsky** - Society of Mind, K-lines (knowledge lines)
   - Book: *The Society of Mind* (1986)
   - Concept: Frames, agents, K-lines as symbolic activators

**Educational Tech:**
4. **Ken Kahn** - ToonTalk, AI blocks for Snap!
   - Book: *The Learner's Apprentice* (generative AI in education)
   - Work: Bringing LLMs into visual programming

5. **Logo** - The turtle graphics language (1967)
   - Papert's educational programming language
   - Learn by commanding a turtle to draw

## **Interactive Fiction & Virtual Worlds**

**Text Adventures:**
11. **Colossal Cave Adventure** (1976) - Will Crowther & Don Woods
    - The first text adventure

12. **Zork** (1977-1979) - Infocom
    - Gold standard parser-based interaction

13. **MUD** (1978) - Richard Bartle & Roy Trubshaw
    - First multi-user dungeon
    - Book: *Designing Virtual Worlds* by Richard Bartle

14. **LambdaMOO** (1990) - Pavel Curtis
    - Social MUD, spatial programming
    - Users building the world while inhabiting it

15. **Habitat** (1986) - Chip Morningstar & Randy Farmer
    - First graphical multiplayer world
    - Paper: "The Lessons of Lucasfilm's Habitat"
    - Where the term "avatar" was coined

**Game Design Theory:**
16. **Warren Robinette** - Atari Adventure (1979), Rocky's Boots (1982)
    - First graphical adventure, Easter eggs, circuit logic games

## **Programming Languages & Systems**

**Prototype-Based:**
17. **Self Language** - David Ungar
    - Prototype inheritance (no classes!)
    - Paper: "It's About Time" - compile when understanding crystallizes
    
18. **JavaScript** - Uses prototype inheritance from Self

**Message Passing:**
19. **Smalltalk** - Alan Kay
    - Everything is an object, everything is a message
    
20. **HyperCard** - Bill Atkinson (1987)
    - Stacks, cards, buttons, end-user programming
    - Revolutionary: non-programmers building interactive systems

21. **HyperLook** - Arthur van Hoff & Don Hopkins
    - HyperCard for NeWS (Sun's PostScript window system)
